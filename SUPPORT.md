# TraceGuard support

Open an issue at https://github.com/librevlad/traceguard/issues with:
- what you did and what you expected;
- the Jira project key and, if it is about one item, the issue key;
- a screenshot of the TraceGuard panel or audit page, without confidential data.

TraceGuard runs on Atlassian Forge and stores nothing outside your Atlassian site; see PRIVACY.md.
